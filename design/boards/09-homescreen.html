A family console home. Chores as game tiles, profiles up top, and achievements that celebrate the little clears.

Why it works on a phone

  • The selected tile is the whole chore at a glance; four profiles stay one tap away.
  • A horizontal row of other quests keeps the family present without crowding the selected tile.
  • Achievements sit above the dock, so the reward is always visible but never in the way.

Built from what exists

  • Tiles are the existing chores; progress is today’s checklist; profiles are the existing family members.
  • Achievements are the one new layer: First Clear, Full House, On a Roll, Perfect Week, and a few extras.
  • No XP, coins, shop, or currency. Achievements derive from completion history; implementing them needs unlock persistence and is post-MVP.

CSS + best fit

  • Original SVG tile art, medals, and console dock; no Nintendo assets, logos, or characters.
  • Dark 10-foot layout at 1440 × 900, with a portrait adaptation for the phone.
  • Playful for game-comfortable kids and still legible for parents.

A family that wants a game-console feel and explicit achievements — the most openly gamified direction.
