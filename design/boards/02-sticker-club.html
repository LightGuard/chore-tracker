Sticker Club

A collectible sticker chart. Chunky cards, animal avatars, and a real reason to finish the list.

Palette / CSS tokens
#FFF9ECCream
#202033Ink
#2F53FFBlue
#FFD447Star
#7BE0B0Mint

Why it works on a phone

  • Four animal avatars replace the child dropdown: one tap, no menu, no sign-in.
  • The chore reads as a collectible card; stars communicate progress before reading.
  • The finish state mints a sticker, adds stars, and teases the sticker book.

CSS-only moves

  • 2px ink borders and 4px offset shadows do most of the work; no illustration library.
  • Star SVGs and emoji avatars scale cleanly; the confetti is a handful of positioned spans.
  • Keep Reshaped Button, Card, and Checkbox behavior and restyle them with new class names.

Best for

Younger kids and reward-driven households. The most playful and immediate of the five.

Phone-first · no new dependencies
