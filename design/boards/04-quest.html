Quest

A co-op game HUD. XP, streaks, and a family leaderboard make chores feel like a daily run.

Why it works on a phone

  • The daily quest is one dark card: title, XP, segmented progress, and a clear next objective.
  • Hex checks and 57px rows are made for thumbs; the active row is a cyan edge, not a badge.
  • Player select becomes a 2×2 card grid, which is faster and more fun than a dropdown.

CSS-only moves

  • Scope a dark theme by overriding --rs-* background, foreground, and border tokens.
  • Clipped card corners, a grid backdrop, and mono labels are pure CSS.
  • Every icon is inline SVG; no icon package or image assets.

Best for

Older kids and teens. It adds streaks and friendly competition without turning chores into a casino.

Phone-first · no new dependencies
