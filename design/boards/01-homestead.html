A warm household ledger. Paper, ink, and a stamped finish — familiar enough to leave on the kitchen counter.

Why it works on a phone

  • A one-tap name pill replaces the select box, so switching kids is a single thumb press.
  • Chore rows are 51px tall with a large stamped checkbox, made for a quick weekly-night check-in.
  • The streak and week strip sit below the chore card, inside natural thumb reach.

CSS-only moves

  • Set --rs-color-brand to terracotta and the page background to warm paper; no images needed.
  • Swap headline fonts to Iowan Old Style and drop card corners to 10px with 1px rules.
  • The “all done” stamp is one rotated, blended element — pure CSS.

Best for

Cozy and legible, with room for personality. Best for a family that wants the app to feel like part of the house.
