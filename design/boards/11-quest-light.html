Why it works on a phone

  • Seven two-letter player cards fit the full 354px width.
  • Six objectives in a 190px window; 3.5 rows visible, the fourth clips.
  • Scrolled state surfaces 03–06 while the HUD and squad stay put.

Built from what exists

  • Seven players and six objectives are the stress test; chores/checklist are unchanged.
  • Light mode is a token swap, not a new data model.
  • The original Quest game layer — XP, streaks, rank — remains post-MVP.

CSS + best fit

  • Cream, black, lime, and cyan; angular cuts and mono labels keep the HUD feel.
  • The desktop window is 268px and shows 5.5 rows; the phone shows 3.5.
  • Best for older kids and teens who want game energy in daylight.
