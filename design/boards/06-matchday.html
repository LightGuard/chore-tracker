Why it works on a phone

  • A one-tap squad strip replaces the child dropdown; every player stays visible.
  • The fixture card makes the chore and the 2/3 score legible at arm’s length.
  • Full time is the payoff: a checkered flag, 3/3, and the next fixture.

Built from what exists

  • The score is today’s checklist; the form guide is the same daily completion history.
  • No XP, rewards, household goals, or leaderboard. No new tables or endpoints.
  • Jersey numbers and the club crest are visual dressing, not stored fields.

CSS + best fit

  • Condensed display type, cream/green/orange, ticket rules, and one checkered-flag SVG.
  • Same Reshaped controls; the profile picker is four buttons instead of a Select.
  • Sport is the shared language — playful for smaller kids, credible for teens.

A family that wants playful, teen-safe energy without adding a reward economy.
