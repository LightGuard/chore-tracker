Why it works on a phone

  • A full-bleed blue top band makes today feel like an event; the cream sheet holds the work.
  • Three checkpoint rows are the whole interaction; the week log is one glance.
  • The bottom “still open” strip is a shortcut to the last checklist item.

Built from what exists

  • 2/3 and the step boxes are the checklist; 2/7 and the week log are existing day completion.
  • No XP, sticker collection, goals, currency, or leaderboard. Nothing new to persist.
  • The report reuses the existing immutable snapshot and its 21/28 math.

CSS + best fit

  • Flat blue/acid blocks, square checks, condensed type, and one hand-drawn note.
  • No image assets or icon package; the sheet overlap is a negative margin and a radius.
  • The playfulness is in the composition, not in extra reward mechanics.

A mixed-age house that wants the most personality without changing what the app tracks.
