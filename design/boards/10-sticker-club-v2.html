Why it works on a phone

  • Seven 46px name tags fit one row; no avatars, no pictograms.
  • Six jobs in a 190px window; long labels wrap and rows grow.
  • The scrolled state keeps the rail and says exactly what is above.

Built from what exists

  • 7 children and 6 checklist items are a deliberate stress test.
  • No person, chore, or subtype pictograms: type, color, checks, and stickers only.
  • The week strip is the existing completion trail; no new fields.

CSS + best fit

  • 2px rules, offset shadows, die-cut corners, and no SVG pictograms.
  • Long labels wrap without breaking the fixed-height window.
  • Best for younger kids and reward-driven houses; sticker rewards stay post-MVP.
